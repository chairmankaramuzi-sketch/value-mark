# Value Mark: Official Valuation Report Format & Structure Reference

Captured from the official Value Mark valuation report sample (Property: Plot 304 Block 23 at Kagugu Cell & Untitled Land at Kyashamire Village, Buyanja Subcounty, Rukungiri District).

---

## 1. Document Sections & Flow

1. **Header & Cover Metadata:**
   - Report Title: `VALUATION REPORT FOR [PROPERTY IDENTIFICATION]`
   - Site/GPS Camera Photographs with embedded coordinates, compass/map overlay, date and time.
2. **Executive Summary Table (Cover Sheet):**
   - Per-parcel values: Market Value (UGX), Forced Sale Value (UGX), Insurance Value (UGX).
   - Key dates: Date of Instruction, Date of Inspection, Date of Valuation.
   - Purpose of Valuation (e.g. Mortgage / Lending Purposes).
   - Client / Instructing Party: Prepared for / Requested by (e.g. BUYANJA SACCO LTD).
   - Reference Number: (e.g. `VMS/JT-AP/077/2025`).
   - Registered Proprietor & Borrower Name + NIN + Contact Number.
   - Inspected in Presence of (e.g. Property Owner / Agent).
   - Property Address & Distance from Main/Tarmac Road.
   - Property Tenure (e.g. Freehold Register Volume/Folio, Customary / Kibanja sales agreement).
   - Property Type, Current Use, Occupancy/Tenancy, Zoning & Land Use.
   - Land Size (Hectares & Acres) + Net Usable Area + Plot Coverage.
   - Boundary Definition (Corner stones, survey markers, hedges/fences).
   - Basis of Value (Market Value) & Method (Market Comparable Approach).
   - GPS Coordinates (per parcel).
   - Valuer & Supervising Registered Surveyor details (SRB Reg No, MISU).
   - Developments & Site Improvements, Built-up Area, Level of Completion.
   - Environmental Concerns & User Restrictions.
   - Encumbrances (Ministry of Lands Zonal Office title search date + local council sales agreement certification).
   - Highest & Best Use analysis.
   - Passing Rent & Estimated Market Rent.
   - Professional Remarks / Special Factors.
   - Suitability for Secured Lending.
   - Summary Values Table with Values in Words.
3. **Limiting Conditions (Standard Legal Terms):**
   - Confidentiality & reliance limited to instructing institution.
   - Authenticity & anti-tamper notice.
4. **Detailed Technical Report:**
   - 1.0 Instructions / Terms of Reference
   - 2.0 Inspection details & attendees (Valuer + Supervising Registered Surveyor)
   - 3.0 Location & Detailed Access Directions (km-by-km turn-by-turn from nearest town roundabout)
   - 3.1 Location Sketch Map & Access Route
   - 3.2 Neighborhood characterization & services
   - 4.0 Cadastral & Site Boundary Drawing (with parcel edge dimensions)
   - 5.0 Basis of Value (IVSC Market Value definition, Forced Sale % policy e.g. 65%, Insurance/Reinstatement)
   - 5.1 Title, Ownership, Tenure, Land Size, Boundaries, Encumbrance search
   - 6.0 Developments & Vegetation
   - 7.0 Services (Water, Power, Roads, Neighborhood Security)
   - 8.0 SWOT Analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats)
   - 9.0 Professional Remarks (Advice on customary risks, encroachment, environmental factors)
   - 10.0 Scope of Valuation
   - 11.0 Highest and Best Use Analysis
   - 12.0 Comparative Market Analysis (Local land price range per acre, applied rate per acre)
   - 13.0 Suitability for Secured Lending statement
   - 14.0 Rating Matrix (Lettability, Salability on 1-7 scale; Market conditions, Vacancies, Tenant, Location)
   - 15.0 Valuation Summary Table (Plot Details, FMV, FSV, Total)
   - 16.0 Final Valuation Conclusion with Amount in Words
   - Sign-off Block: Value Mark Surveyors, Valuer credentials, SRB Reg No, Signature & Date
5. **Photo Appendices:**
   - Property overview photographs
   - Neighborhood & access road photographs
   - Google Earth satellite overview with marked travel route from town centre
